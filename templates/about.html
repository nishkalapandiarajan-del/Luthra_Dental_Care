MEET OUR DOCTOR

Dr. Kousalya Rajaram

BDS | Cert.RCT & Founder

Dr. Kousalya Rajaram is a dedicated Dental Surgeon (BDS) with over 12 years of clinical experience in providing comprehensive dental care. With additional certification in Root Canal Treatment, she has a special interest in endodontic procedures and restoring natural teeth.

She believes in delivering ethical, patient-centred dental care with a gentle approach, careful diagnosis and personalised treatment planning. Her goal is to make every patient feel comfortable and confident while helping them achieve healthy, beautiful smiles.

At Luthra Dental Care, she is committed to providing quality dental treatment in a friendly, comfortable and caring environment.
