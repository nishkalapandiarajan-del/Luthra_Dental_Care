HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our dental care and services.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Appointments are recommended so we can give you dedicated time and reduce waiting. Walk-in patients are also welcome, subject to availability. For a convenient visit, please call or WhatsApp us before coming.

What are your clinic hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday, from 9:30 AM to 1:00 PM and 5:00 PM to 8:30 PM. The clinic remains closed on Sundays.

Do you treat children?

Yes! We welcome children and provide dental care with a gentle, patient-friendly approach. Regular dental check-ups can help children develop healthy oral hygiene habits from an early age.

Is teeth cleaning or scaling painful?

Professional teeth cleaning and scaling are generally comfortable. You may experience mild sensitivity or slight discomfort, especially if your gums are sensitive. Our team takes care to make the procedure as comfortable as possible.

Are your instruments properly sterilized?

Patient safety and hygiene are important to us. Dental instruments are cleaned and sterilized using appropriate procedures between patients, and disposable items are used wherever necessary.

What payment options do you accept?

Please contact our clinic directly to confirm the currently available payment options. Our team will be happy to guide you before your visit.

How do I know which treatment is right for me?

The right treatment depends on your dental condition and individual needs. Our dentist will examine your teeth and gums, understand your concerns, and explain the suitable treatment options before proceeding.

How often should I visit the dentist?

Regular dental check-ups are generally recommended every six months. However, your dentist may suggest more frequent visits depending on your oral health and treatment needs.

Do you offer emergency dental care?

If you experience severe tooth pain, swelling, a broken tooth, or a dental injury, please contact us as soon as possible. We will guide you regarding the next available appointment and appropriate care.

How can I book, reschedule, or cancel an appointment?

You can call us at 82700 50287 or contact us through WhatsApp to book, reschedule, or cancel your appointment. Please inform us in advance if your plans change.
